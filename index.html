About

I am a computational and theoretical physicist specializing in the intersection of geometry, multidimensional optimization, and first-principles electronic structure modeling. I recently completed my Ph.D. in Physics at Pennsylvania State University, where my doctoral research centered on formulating mathematical frameworks and developing scalable algorithms to solve complex energy surface problems.

Most recently, I derived and developed NEMpy, an open-source Python package that extends Nudged Elastic Band (NEB) methods into multi-dimensional Nudged Elastic Membrane (NEM) algorithms. By integrating NEMpy with density functional theory (DFT) engines such as Quantum ESPRESSO and modern machine learning potentials, my research enables automated mapping of adsorbate interactions and minimum energy pathways across complex material interfaces.

Beyond computational algorithm development, my background includes extensive research across quasi-classical models of quantum dynamics, gravitational lensing, and autonomous robotics. I am passionate about constructing robust, open-source numerical tools that leverage modern computational frameworks (such as NumPy, pandas, ASE, and PyMatGen) to accelerate the discovery and optimization of advanced functional materials.
