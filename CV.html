Research Experience Dates

Computational Algorithm Development for Energy Materials 6/24-8/26

Pennsylvania State University, Department of Physics

  • Constructed novel proof of the existence of two-dimensional minimum energy surfaces, demonstrating that the problem is well-posed, with unique solutions.
  • Derived and constructed a novel nudged elastic membrane (NEM) algorithm in python, NEMpy, for approximating minimum energy surfaces of systems with many degrees of freedom. Modeled as an extension of the nudged elastic band (NEB) technique with finite elastic elements. Demonstrated compatibility with Quantum ESPRESSO and machine learning potentials.
  • Estimated energies of adsorbates on surfaces of novel materials at vacuum interfaces using Quantum ESPRESSO. Systems modeled using ASE and PyMatGen; data analyzed with pandas and NumPy. Created bash scripts to automate input file generation and extract output data.

Euclideanization of Minkowski Spacetime 12/22-6/24

Pennsylvania State University, Department of Physics

  • Constructed a novel transformation from the hyperbolic geometry of special relativity to Euclidean space. Useful for computing integrals in quantum field theory.

Quasi-classical Models of Quantum Dynamics 8/19-5/23

Pennsylvania State University, Department of Physics

  • Estimated tunneling times in quantum double-well potentials via a newly published quasi-classical model of quantum dynamics, based on moments of operators.
  • Derived a novel mapping from expectation values of operators obeying the su(3) lie algebra to canonical coordinates based on Poisson brackets.

Complex Formulation of Gravitational Lensing 5/14-5/17

University of South Florida, Department of Mathematics & Statistics

  • Found classes of solutions for locations of images formed by gravitational lenses of a particular geometry.
  • Created lensing simulations to explore a broader range of system parameters than could be handled analytically.

RoboBulls: Soccer-Playing Robots 1/14-12/16

University of South Florida, Student Organization
